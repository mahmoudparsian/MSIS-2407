# Data Modeling — Further Reading

[← Week 1](../README.md) · [Course home](../../README.md)

Optional background reading for Week 1 (and Week 2's relational model). These
are third-party materials, so they are linked here rather than copied into
this repository. Start with the first two if you're new to data modeling.

| Reading | By | Length | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Data Modeling 101: A Beginner's Guide to Success](https://www.thedataschool.com.au/blogs/data-modeling-101-a-beginners-guide-to-success/) | The Data School Down Under (2024) | Short article | Conceptual, logical and physical models; keys; crow's-foot relationships |
| [A beginner's guide to relational databases](https://medium.com/@sumit_m/a-beginners-guide-to-relational-databases-d1bdd05beba2) | Sumit M., Medium (2025) | 7-minute read | Tables, keys, consistency and why relational design matters in practice |
| [Chapter 2: The Relational Model](https://www.cs.uct.ac.za/mit_notes/database/pdfs/chp02.pdf) | University of Cape Town, database course notes | 37 pages (PDF) | Relations, attributes, domains, tuples, primary and foreign keys, integrity constraints, relational algebra |
| [Data Modeling 101: Getting Started](https://2017.code4lib.org/workshops/Data-Modeling-101-Getting-Started) ([workshop document](https://bit.ly/C4LDataModeling101)) | Code4Lib 2017 workshop | Workshop materials | Data modeling for libraries, archives and museums — a different domain with the same ideas |
| *Learn Data Modelling by Example* (Part 1 Beginner, Part 2 Intermediate) | [Barry Williams](https://www.smashwords.com/profile/view/Barry_Williams) — see also his [Database Answers](http://www.databaseanswers.org/) library of ~950 example data models | Book series | Worked models of real organizations, from simple to intermediate |

**Tip:** while reading, sketch the entities and relationships for one example
yourself before looking at the author's diagram — the same habit the Week 1
labs build.
